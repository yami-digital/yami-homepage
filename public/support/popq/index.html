01 Getting Started

Pop Q is a set of question cards you read aloud to your kid — there's nothing to set up.

  • Tap Next (or swipe left) for a new question
  • Swipe right to go back to the last question
  • Tap the age-band chip in the corner to switch between ages 3-5, 6-9, and 10-12
  • Tap Think deeper when a question lands and you want a follow-up prompt

02 Frequently Asked Questions

Is my child's data safe?

Yes — there's nothing to keep safe in the first place. Pop Q makes no network requests at all; everything stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Why do questions repeat?

Pop Q avoids repeating a question you've just seen, but with a fixed set of questions per age band, you'll eventually cycle back around — that's expected, not a bug. We're actively adding more questions.

The app froze or won't open — what do I do?

Force-quit Pop Q (swipe up and hold, then swipe the app away) and reopen it. If that doesn't fix it, email us with your iPhone model and iOS version and we'll look into it.

Can I suggest a question?

Yes, please — email us. Question ideas from real parents and kids are exactly what makes Pop Q better.

How do I change the age band?

Tap the age-band chip in the corner of the card and pick a different one — it takes effect immediately.
