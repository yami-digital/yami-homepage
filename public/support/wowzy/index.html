Wowzy Support

Help with Wowzy: Family Rewards, the reward chart where kids earn points for wows, pick rewards, and get a fresh start each week.

Applies to: Wowzy iOS app  ·  Contact: dev@yamidigital.com

Need help?
  • ✉️ Email dev@yamidigital.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.
  • 📱 Tell us your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version and Wowzy version (App Store > Wowzy).
  • 🔒 Please don't send your parent PIN. We never need it.

01 Getting Started

  • Open Wowzy and follow the short setup: pick what to track and add your kids' names. Starter lists are filled in, and you can edit everything later in the Plan tab.
  • Wows earn points. Rewards spend them, today or ordered for a later day. A don't asks for one give-up from a list you agree on, and points are never taken away.
  • Limits (per day, per week or per weekday) reset on your family's start day: Plan > Plan Settings.

02 Parent PIN and Kid Mode

  • Set a PIN: Plan > Plan Settings (gear) > Parent Lock > Set Parent PIN. With a PIN, logging and edits ask for it; an unlock lasts 5 minutes. Face ID or Touch ID can unlock instead.
  • Kid mode: on Home, tap your kid's name (top left) > Hand to <name>. Kids can see their points, ask for a wow and claim rewards. To leave, tap the name (lock icon) and enter the PIN.
  • Forgot the PIN? On the PIN pad, tap Forgot PIN? and confirm with your iPhone passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, then choose a new PIN.

03 Backups and a New Phone

  • Your family's plan and history are stored only on your device. They are included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or an encrypted backup on your computer).
  • Moving to a new iPhone: restore the new phone from a backup of the old one, and Wowzy comes back with all its history.
  • Deleting the app deletes its data. Make a backup first if you want to keep it.
The parent PIN is kept in this device's Keychain and is not restored to another device; set it again after moving.

04 Common Questions

  • Why is an item greyed out? It reached its limit for today or this week, or it isn't available today. The card says why, and it comes back after the reset.
  • I logged the wrong thing. Tap Undo right after, or open History from Home to undo any entry.
  • Can two parents use it? Today Wowzy works on one device. Sharing between devices is on the way.
  • Does Wowzy collect data? No. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no data collected. See the privacy policy.
