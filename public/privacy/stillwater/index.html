StillWater
keeps your data yours.

StillWater is built on a simple principle: what you write is private. No servers, no accounts, no tracking. Your reflections live on your device and nowhere else.

Last updated: June 4, 2026  ·  Applies to: StillWater iOS app

TL;DR
  • ✅ Your journal entries and emotion logs are stored locally on your device only
  • ✅ Face ID is used only for local app lock — biometric data never leaves your device
  • ✅ No accounts, no backend, no analytics, no ads
  • ✅ No third-party SDKs — zero external data sharing
  • ✅ Future iCloud sync routes through your own Apple account — we never see it

01 What StillWater Stores

StillWater stores the following data locally on your device, using SwiftData in your app's private on-device storage:

  • Journal entries you write
  • Emotion logs and mood check-ins
  • App preferences and settings (e.g. theme, lock preferences)
stored on-device never transmitted no cloud by default
Age restriction: StillWater is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

02 What We Don't Collect

We collect nothing. There is no backend, no database, and no server that receives your data. Specifically, StillWater has:

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising networks
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No data brokers, no data selling, ever
no analytics no ads no accounts no backend
Apple crash diagnostics: When StillWater is distributed via TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may provide anonymized crash logs through App Store Connect. These logs contain device type, OS version, and a stack trace — they are not linked to any personal identity and are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

03 Face ID & Biometric Lock

StillWater offers an optional Face ID / Touch ID lock to keep your entries private on a shared device. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by iOS — StillWater never accesses, processes, or stores your biometric data. It is never transmitted anywhere.

How it works: When you enable the app lock, iOS checks your biometric against its own secure enclave and returns only a pass/fail result to StillWater. Your Face ID or fingerprint data never enters the app.

04 iCloud Sync (coming soon)

A future update will offer optional iCloud sync so your entries are available across your Apple devices. This feature is opt-in and works as follows:

  • Data syncs through your own Apple iCloud account
  • YAMI Digital Lab has no access to your iCloud data at any point
  • Apple's Privacy Policy governs how iCloud stores and protects your data
  • You can disable iCloud sync at any time from iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → StillWater
opt-in only your iCloud account we never see it

05 Third-Party Services

StillWater contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, and no analytics services. The only external dependency is Apple's own iOS frameworks, which are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, share, or monetize your data in any form — now or in the future.

California residents (CCPA): We do not sell, and have never sold, personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). There is no personal information to sell — none is collected.

Note: Apple provides aggregate, non-personal install and download metrics in App Store Connect (e.g. total downloads by region). This data is anonymized, not linked to individuals, and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

06 Deleting Your Data

Since all data is stored locally, you have full control:

  • Delete individual entries — from within the app at any time
  • Delete everything — uninstall StillWater from your device
  • Delete iCloud data (once sync is available) — iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → StillWater

07 Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here with an updated date. For significant changes we'll include a notice in the app. Continuing to use StillWater after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
