Wowzy
keeps your family's plan at home.

Wowzy helps a parent and their kids agree on a family plan: points for wows, a reward shop, and a calm fresh start after a slip. Everything lives on the parent's device. There are no accounts, no servers, and no network connection — we never receive any of it.

Last updated: October 23, 2026  ·  Applies to: Wowzy iOS app

TL;DR
  • ✅ Your family plan and activity log are stored on your device only, encrypted by iOS
  • ✅ Wowzy makes zero network requests — no accounts, no backend, no analytics, no ads
  • ✅ No third-party SDKs — nothing is shared with anyone
  • ✅ Nothing is collected from anyone, including children
  • ✅ The parent owns and controls all data; deleting the app deletes it

01 What Wowzy Stores

Wowzy stores the following locally on your device only, in the app's private storage:

  • Your family plan — the lists and items you set up (wows, rewards, don'ts, give-ups), their points and rules, and plan settings such as the reset period and time zone, including earlier versions of the plan
  • The activity log — each wow logged, reward redeemed, don't, give-up, attempt, undo, and fresh start, with its date and time
  • A setup marker — whether first-launch setup has been finished

That's the entire list. Names of lists and items are whatever your family types in; Wowzy doesn't ask for anyone's name, age, photo, contact details, or location. The files are protected with iOS Data Protection (encrypted at rest, unreadable after a restart until the device is first unlocked).

stored on-device never transmitted encrypted by iOS
Device backups: Like other app data, Wowzy's data is included in your own iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on. Those backups belong to your Apple account and are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy — YAMI Digital Lab has no access to them.

02 What We Don't Collect

We collect nothing. Wowzy makes no network requests at all, and there is no backend, database, or server that could receive your data. Specifically, Wowzy has:

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier (IDFA) or cross-app tracking
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location access
  • No links out of the app and no in-app purchases
  • No data brokers, no data selling, ever
no analytics no ads no accounts no network access
Apple crash diagnostics: When Wowzy is distributed via TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may provide anonymized crash logs through App Store Connect if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings. These logs contain device type, OS version, and a stack trace — they are not linked to any personal identity and are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

03 Children's Privacy

Wowzy is a parent's tool that families use together with their children, typically ages 6–12. The app lives on the parent's device, and children use it with a parent, under the parent's supervision — for example, to see their points or pick a reward.

Wowzy does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. There is no sign-up, no profile, and no way for information entered in the app to leave the device, so YAMI Digital Lab never receives a child's (or a parent's) personal information. Because no personal information is collected, there is no data collection under COPPA (the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) or similar children's privacy laws that would require parental consent.

  • Parents control all data. Everything Wowzy stores is on the parent's device and can be viewed and changed there.
  • Parents can delete it at any time by deleting the app (see below).
  • If we ever add a feature that would send data off the device (such as syncing between parents), we will update this policy first and ask for a parent's permission in the app before anything is shared.
Questions about your child's privacy? Email us at dev@yamidigital.com.

04 Third-Party Services

Wowzy contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, and no analytics services. The only external dependency is Apple's own iOS frameworks, which are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, share, or monetize your data in any form — now or in the future.

California residents (CCPA): We do not sell, and have never sold, personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). There is no personal information to sell — none is collected.

Note: Apple provides aggregate, non-personal install and download metrics in App Store Connect (e.g. total downloads by region). This data is anonymized, not linked to individuals, and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

05 Deleting Your Data

Since all data is stored on your device, you have full control:

  • Change the plan — edit or remove lists, items, and rules in the app at any time
  • Undo an entry — undo or correct logged activity from History
  • Delete everything — delete Wowzy from your device; this removes the plan and the whole activity log
  • Backups — copies in your own iPhone backups can be managed in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Backups

There's no account to delete, because none exists.
