Pop Q
collects nothing to begin with.

Pop Q is a set of question cards a parent reads aloud to their kid — there's no login, no network connection, and nothing for a child to type, record, or submit. There's almost nothing here to have a policy about, but here it is in full anyway.

Last updated: October 23, 2026  ·  Applies to: Pop Q iOS app

TL;DR
  • ✅ Pop Q makes zero network requests — the app is fully offline, always
  • ✅ The only things stored are an age-band preference and a short list of recently-shown question IDs, both on your device only
  • ✅ No accounts, no backend, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs
  • ✅ No text field, microphone, or camera anywhere in the app — a child cannot type, speak into, or submit anything to Pop Q
  • ✅ Built for ages 3–12, used together with a parent or guardian who holds the device

01 What Pop Q Stores

Pop Q stores exactly two things, locally on your device only, using standard iOS app storage:

  • Which age band is selected (ages 3–5, 6–9, or 10–12) — so questions match who's playing
  • A short rolling list of recently-shown question IDs — so the same question doesn't repeat right away

That's the entire list. Pop Q has no text field, chat box, voice input, or any other way for a child (or anyone) to type, speak, or submit content into the app — the questions are pre-written and built into the app itself; nothing about what's said out loud in response is ever captured, recorded, or stored by Pop Q.

stored on-device never transmitted no conversation content stored

02 What We Don't Collect

Pop Q makes no network requests at all — not at launch, not in the background, ever. There is no backend, no database, and no server that could receive your data even if the app wanted to send it. Specifically, Pop Q has:

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising networks
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No microphone, camera, or location access
  • No data brokers, no data selling, ever
no analytics no ads no accounts no network access
Apple crash diagnostics: When Pop Q is distributed via TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may provide anonymized crash logs through App Store Connect. These logs contain device type, OS version, and a stack trace — they are not linked to any personal identity and are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

03 Children's Privacy

Pop Q is designed for children ages 3–12, used together with a parent or guardian who holds the device and asks the questions out loud — the child doesn't operate the app directly.

Pop Q does not collect any personal information from a child, or from anyone. There's no sign-up, no text field for a child to type into, and no microphone or camera access — there is simply no mechanism in the app through which a child's personal information could be collected, with or without a parent's involvement. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing a parent needs to consent to under COPPA (the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) or similar children's privacy laws — the app has no data-collection mechanism for those laws to apply to in the first place.

If you have questions about how Pop Q handles your child's privacy beyond what's written here, reach out any time — see Contact below.

04 Third-Party Services

Pop Q contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, and no analytics services. The only external dependency is Apple's own iOS frameworks, which are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, share, or monetize your data in any form — now or in the future.

California residents (CCPA): We do not sell, and have never sold, personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). There is no personal information to sell — none is collected.

Note: Apple provides aggregate, non-personal install and download metrics in App Store Connect (e.g. total downloads by region). This data is anonymized, not linked to individuals, and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

05 Deleting Your Data

Since the only data Pop Q stores lives on your device, you have full control:

  • Reset the age band or shown-question history — not currently exposed as an in-app option; uninstalling clears it
  • Delete everything — uninstall Pop Q from your device

There's no account to delete, because none exists.
